01 — Electricity

The day-ahead curve

NL day-ahead prices are published once daily by ENTSO-E for delivery the next day, in hourly blocks. They swing with demand and, increasingly, with solar — on sunny early afternoons oversupply has pushed the price below zero 0 times in the last — days.

02 — Gas

The futures proxy

Note

There is no free, officially published day-ahead auction for TTF gas, and no hourly TTF price exists at all. What's charted below is Yahoo Finance's delayed quote for the TTF front-month calendar future (ticker TTF=F, ICE Endex) — a close proxy for near-term gas prices, not the literal ICE Endex Day-Ahead index.

Sources & automation

Electricity prices come from the ENTSO-E Transparency Platform (NL day-ahead auction, document type A44), fetched daily around 13:30 UTC. Gas prices come from Yahoo Finance's TTF=F quote, fetched daily around 20:00 UTC. Both runs are scheduled GitHub Actions that commit straight back into the repo — there is no server behind this page.

Elsewhere

Prices are delayed, informational, and not trading advice. "Prijspeil" borrows its name from the peilschaal — the level gauges Dutch waterways are read against — applied here to a price instead of a canal.
